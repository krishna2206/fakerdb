Four passes instead of one prompt

A single prompt has to invent every row at once, so by row 300 it has forgotten the IDs it wrote at row 20. The agent splits the job into passes and checks its own work.

  1. 1

    Plan

    Reads your tables, resolves the foreign-key graph, catches cycles, and decides what has to exist before what.

  2. 2

    Generate

    Fills parent tables first, in batches small enough to stay consistent. Child rows only reference IDs that were actually written.

  3. 3

    Validate

    Tests every key, unique index and check constraint, plus rules it infers from your columns, like dates in order and totals that add up.

  4. 4

    Repair

    Rewrites only the rows that failed and checks them again. The script you download runs top to bottom with every constraint on.

Everything between an empty database and a real one

Most seeders hand you user_1@example.com a thousand times. FakerDB hands you data your queries, charts and demos can be tested against.

Visual schema editor

Drag tables onto the canvas and draw a line between two columns to create a foreign key. Keys, uniques and defaults are set right on the node.

Schema from a sentence

Describe the app, for example “a booking platform with venues, slots and reviews”, and get a normalised schema you can edit before any rows exist.

Rows that mean something

A customer in France gets a French name, phone number and postcode. Order totals match their line items. Shipping dates come after order dates.

Big datasets, never truncated

Batches keep each step small, so 100,000 rows come out as complete as 100. No statement cut off halfway, no repeated tail.

Export anywhere

A runnable .sql script, a zip of per-table CSVs, or a block to paste. Everything is ordered to import with every constraint switched on.

Synthetic by design

No connection to your database, no production data, no training on your schema. Enterprise plans run inside your own infrastructure.

Common questions

When can I use the agent version?

We're building it now. Join early access and we'll email you when it opens. Until then, v1 is free to use at fakerdb.vercel.app with your own API key. It generates the schema and rows in a single prompt.

How is this different from asking a chatbot for INSERT statements?

A chatbot writes everything in one pass. By row 300 it has lost track of the IDs it wrote at row 20, and long outputs stop mid-statement. FakerDB plans the insert order, generates in batches, then validates every key and constraint and repairs failing rows before you download anything.

Do I need my own AI API key?

Not with the agent version. Generation will run on FakerDB's servers and be covered by your credits, so you never configure a provider or get a separate bill from one. v1 still asks for your own key.

How are credits consumed?

Credits follow the real cost of a run, which depends mostly on the number of rows and the width of your tables. As a rule of thumb, one credit covers about a hundred rows. The app shows an estimate before you run and the exact cost after.

Does FakerDB connect to my database?

Never. You give it a schema, typed, drawn or pasted, and it gives you a script. Nothing runs against your servers, and no connection string is stored.

Is my schema used to train models?

No. Your schemas and datasets stay in your project, are not used for training, and are deleted with your account. Enterprise plans can run entirely inside your own infrastructure.

Can I import an existing database?

Yes. Paste your CREATE TABLE statements and FakerDB rebuilds the diagram, foreign keys included, so you can generate data for a schema you already run in production.

What happens when I run out of credits?

Generation pauses. Your projects, schemas and previous datasets stay untouched and exportable. Top up instantly, or wait for the next monthly refill.

Can I cancel or change plan?

Any time, in one click, from the billing page. Downgrades take effect at the end of the current period, and you keep your remaining credits until they expire.
